OUR BEGINNINGS

A Heritage of Faith and Remembrance

Mehfil-e-Murtazvi was established in 1959 in Nindo Shaher to promote religious gatherings and the remembrance of Imam Hussain (A.S.). It was formally registered in 1962, marking an important milestone in the organization's history.

The organization emerged from a tradition of community participation in religious gatherings, particularly during the first ten days of Muharram and on the 12th of Muharram. These gatherings reflected a shared commitment to faith, remembrance, and the preservation of religious traditions.

OUR PURPOSE

Preserving a Meaningful Tradition

The heritage of Mehfil-e-Murtazvi represents devotion, collective remembrance, and the importance of bringing people together through religious gatherings. Its history remains an important part of the community's cultural and spiritual identity.

A CONTINUING LEGACY

Faith, Unity, and Remembrance

The story of Mehfil-e-Murtazvi is rooted in the dedication of those who helped establish and sustain its traditions. As we reflect on its history, we also recognize the importance of preserving this heritage for future generations.
