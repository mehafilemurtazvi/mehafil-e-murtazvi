Our Religious Activities

Preserving the remembrance of Imam Hussain (A.S.), honouring the message of Karbala, and bringing the community together through faith and tradition.

OUR COMMITMENT

Faith, Remembrance & Unity

Mehfil-e-Murtazvi, Nindo Shaher, has a longstanding tradition of religious gatherings and remembrance. Our activities reflect devotion to Imam Hussain (A.S.) and the enduring message of Karbala.

01

Majalis-e-Aza

Religious gatherings dedicated to the remembrance of Imam Hussain (A.S.), reflection on the events of Karbala, and the lessons of sacrifice, truth, patience, and justice.

02

Muharram Commemorations

Commemorative gatherings during Muharram that honour the sacrifice of Imam Hussain (A.S.) and preserve the religious traditions passed down through generations.

03

Religious Gatherings

Gatherings that provide opportunities for the community to come together in remembrance, reflection, and the continuation of the traditions of Mehfil-e-Murtazvi.

04

Community Participation

Encouraging community participation and intergenerational involvement to help preserve the heritage, values, and traditions of Mehfil-e-Murtazvi for the future.
