Our Story

Introduction to Mehfil-e-Murtazvi, Nindo Shaher

Mehfil-e-Murtazvi was established in 1959 to promote religious gatherings and the remembrance of Imam Hussain (A.S.). Its historical account refers to formal registration in 1962.

The organization emerged at a time when young people organized gatherings during the first ten days of Muharram, particularly on the 12th of Muharram, to commemorate the martyrdom of Imam Hussain (A.S.) and his companions.

From these early gatherings, Mehfil-e-Murtazvi developed into an organization dedicated to religious devotion, remembrance, and the preservation of community traditions.

The Beginning

Establishment and Founding Members

On the 12th of Muharram 1959, a group of young people established Mehfil-e-Murtazvi in Nindo Shaher. Their commitment helped lay the foundation for a tradition of religious gatherings and remembrance that has continued across generations.

The founding members included:

  • The late Syed Ibrahim Shah
  • The late Ali Khawaja
  • The late Haji Muhammad Sumar
  • Sadiq Ali Khawaja
  • Dr. Muzmil Hussain
  • Muhammad Suleman Jafri
  • Other respected community members

Initially organized by young people, the gathering gradually developed into a formal organization. Members and office-bearers continued working together to preserve its religious and community traditions.

Our Purpose

Aims and Objectives

Mehfil-e-Murtazvi was established with the fundamental objective of commemorating the martyrdom of Imam Hussain (A.S.) and promoting the timeless message of Karbala.

The organization seeks to encourage young people to participate in religious activities, maintain discipline and unity, and preserve the traditions of mourning, known as Azadari.

Its key objectives include:

  • Commemorating the sacrifices of Imam Hussain (A.S.) and his companions.
  • Promoting the message and values of Karbala.
  • Preserving the traditions of Azadari during Muharram.
  • Encouraging youth participation in religious gatherings.
  • Strengthening unity, discipline, and community involvement.
  • Honouring the Ahl-e-Bait (A.S.) and their sacrifices.

Over the years, the organization has continued its activities with a commitment to religious devotion and respect for Imam Hussain (A.S.) and his companions.
